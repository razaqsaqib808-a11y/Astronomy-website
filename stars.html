Stars

From tiny red dwarfs to massive blue giants – the engines of the universe.

The Sun

G2V Yellow Dwarf

5,778 K • 1 M⊙ • 1 R⊙

Our life-giving star. Powers Earth for 4.6 billion years. Will become a white dwarf in 5 billion years.

Sirius

A1V White Star

9,940 K • 2.02 M⊙ • 1.71 R⊙

Brightest star in the night sky. Actually a binary system with Sirius B, a white dwarf companion.

Betelgeuse

M1-2Ia Red Supergiant

3,500 K • 11.6 M⊙ • 887 R⊙

Will explode as a supernova within 100,000 years. Visible from 640 light-years away.

Rigel

B8Ia Blue Supergiant

12,100 K • 21 M⊙ • 78.9 R⊙

One of the most luminous stars. 120,000 times brighter than the Sun.

Proxima Centauri

M5.5Ve Red Dwarf

3,042 K • 0.122 M⊙ • 0.154 R⊙

Closest star to the Sun (4.24 light-years). Hosts potentially habitable exoplanet.

Vega

A0V White Star

9,602 K • 2.14 M⊙ • 2.36 R⊙

Used as a standard for stellar brightness. Has a debris disk – possible planet formation.

The Lifecycle of a Star

Nebula

A nebula is a cloud of gas and dust where new stars are born.

Protostar

Gravity pulls the nebula's gas together to form a protostar.

Main Sequence

The star fuses hydrogen into helium, shining steadily for billions of years.

Red Giant / Supergiant

Stars expand and cool after hydrogen fuel runs out, becoming giants or supergiants.

Supernova

The star explodes, scattering elements into space, forming new stars and planets.

Neutron Star / Black Hole

Depending on its mass, the star leaves behind a neutron star or a black hole.

Interesting Facts About Stars

Stars are born in nebulae, massive clouds of gas and dust in space.

The closest star to Earth is the Sun, which is a medium-sized main sequence star.

Some stars are so massive that they burn out quickly, living only a few million years.

A teaspoon of a neutron star would weigh around a billion tons on Earth.

The largest known stars, called supergiants, are over 1,000 times larger than the Sun.

Stars create heavier elements through nuclear fusion, which are the building blocks for planets and life.
