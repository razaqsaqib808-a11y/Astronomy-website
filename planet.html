Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System. It has no atmosphere and extreme temperature variations.

Venus

Venus is similar in size to Earth but has a toxic atmosphere mostly of carbon dioxide and surface temperatures hot enough to melt lead.

Earth

Earth is the only planet known to support life, with water covering 71% of its surface and a breathable atmosphere of nitrogen and oxygen.

Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface. It has the tallest volcano and a canyon system bigger than Earth's.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System, a gas giant with a strong magnetic field and dozens of moons including Europa and Ganymede.

Saturn

Saturn is famous for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock. It is a gas giant with at least 82 moons.

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green color due to methane in its atmosphere. It rotates on its side and has faint rings.

Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun, an ice giant with strong winds and storms. Triton is its largest moon.
