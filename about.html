About Astro Universe

At Astro Universe, we are passionate about exploring the mysteries of space. From galaxies far beyond to the smallest particles of stardust, our mission is to make the cosmos accessible and fascinating for everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ignite curiosity about the universe. Through this project by EPTech, we aim to share the wonders of astronomy with everyone, bringing cosmic discoveries, facts, and visuals closer to all dreamers on Earth.

Our Vision

Our vision is to bring the universe closer to everyone. Through this project, we aim to merge science and creativity, allowing new generations to explore planets, stars, and galaxies with engaging digital experiences.

Our Team

This astronomy project was assigned to our team by Eptech. Our dedicated members — Saqib Razaq, Faizan Arain, and Ahmed Shabir — collaborated to create this digital space experience, combining science, design, and creativity.
